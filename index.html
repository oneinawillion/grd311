Treats

For this section, we would like to share one of our most popular halloween/fall sweet treat recipe! This is a fall brownie recipe. Here's what you'll need and the steps to make it:

  • 1/2 cup butter, or oil
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa, preferably, dutch processed
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 1/4 tsp salt, leave out if using salted butter
  • 1/2 cup favorite adds-ins , (chopped dark chocolate, chocolate chips or walnut)
  1. Pre-heat oven to 350F. Grease an 8x8 square pan or a 9x5 loaf pan or line with parchment or foil; set aside.
  2. In a large microwave-safe mixing bowl, add the butter and microwave for 1 minute or until completely melted. Add the sugar and cocoa powder and whisk vigorously for at least a minute, until the sugar has dissolved.
  3. Add eggs one at a time then vanilla and whisk for at least 1 minute, until well combined and batter is shiny.
  4. Stir in flour and salt until the flour is fully combined. Be careful not to overmix mix.
  5. (optional) fold in 1/2 cup of nuts, raisins, chocolate chips or anything you desire.
  6. Spread in pan and bake for approximately 20 minutes or until the center is slightly set. Be careful not to over-bake!
  7. Cool completely then cut into 9 large squares or 16 small squares ( I cut mine into 16)

Games

Wanna have some fun competition with your friends while being outdoors? Let me introduce you to cornhole! Here's how to play:

  • Players use an underhand toss, alternating throws with their opponent until all 8 bags are thrown in a round.
  • A bag that lands and remains on the board at the end of the round.
  • Cornhole (3 points): A bag that passes through the hole (or hangs in the hole supported by the board).
  • Dead Bag (0 points): Any bag that hits the ground before landing on the board, or touches both the ground and the board.
  • Cancellation Scoring: Points cancel each other out, so only one team scores per round based on the point difference between the opponents.
  • Winning: The first team to reach or exceed 21 points at the end of a round wins the game.

Make sure all of the people playing still get along after the game ends because it can get competitive
